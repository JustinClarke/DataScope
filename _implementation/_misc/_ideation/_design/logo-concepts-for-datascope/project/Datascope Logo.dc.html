Where each one wins
1a is the most literal "datascope" — a scope framing a join graph. 1b is the strongest app icon: one letter, holds up at 16px, feels like a shutter that opens only for you. 1c says columnar/DuckDB-Wasm loudest but is the most generic category-wise. 1d is the safest product-marketing mark — instantly "query results".
